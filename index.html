FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Lash lift or lash extensions?

A lash lift curls and tints your own lashes, with nothing added, and lasts about 6 to 8 weeks. Extensions add length and fullness and usually last through a whole holiday. Not sure? We'll help you choose.

Is it gentle on my natural lashes?

We work gently and check how you feel throughout. A lift doesn't add anything to your lashes, and our extensions are light and comfortable to wear.

Do I need to book?

Booking is the best way to get the time you want, especially in high season. Choose a time online and we'll confirm on WhatsApp or Zalo.
